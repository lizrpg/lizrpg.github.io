Cirrusland's Most Famous Pastry

Cirrus Puff Copycat

Hello to my whimsical, wonderful witches! I have just been dying to pass along my copycat recipe for Tropo Puffs, the famous pastry from the cloud kingdom of Stratusmoor! All of my regular readers might be wondering: "We thought you were vacationing with Simon in the fifth circle? How on earth did you end up in Stratusmoor, where no Demon Shall Ever Step Hoof?!”

Why, if it weren't for Simon's gracious, selfless nature I would not have had such a wonderful opportunity! You see, Simon was on holiday in a lovely little hamlet in the fifth circle, so I divined his location to surprise him! While he was taken aback at first, he immediately appreciated my company and we spent two incredible nights together in the most precious cottage you can imagine. Garlands of sulfurflowers hang around the door, tallow candles perch on every surface, and the fantastic glow of the saffron sky fills the canted bay window.

On the morning of the third day, Simon approached me with an adorable nervousness. He handed me a flyer and a sky blue ticket, singed around the edges, for the event of a lifetime. Cirrusland, the Biggest Fair in the Known Universe, was coming to Stratusmoor!-- their first visit in one hundred years! And my beloved Simon had risked hoof and horn to get me a ticket.

Suffice to say, my dears, I was shocked! Incubi have long been known as mercurial and selfish creatures, but my Simon proves the opposite to be the case. How it must have broken his heart to send me away from our cozy little paradise in the fifth. Tearfully, I departed on my broom and Rose Up into the Skylands.

I detailed the whole of my visit to Stratusmoor and Cirrusland on my blog, but I know you are all here for the Tropo Puffs, so I won't delay any longer ;) Purported to be the lightest pastry with the richest custardy filling in all of the Skylands, I was afraid that this little cream puff would not live up to its reputation. I am happy to announce that it not only lived up to its reputation, it exceeded it. After the first bite I knew that I had to try my hand at a copycat once I returned to my hut in Bracken Fen.

After just five failed experiments, I think that I got as close to a Tropo Puff as possible. Without further ado:
